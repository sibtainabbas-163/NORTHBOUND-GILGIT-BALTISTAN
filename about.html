A SENSE OF PLACE   ·   01
WHY NORTHBOUND01 — 03

Not just a destination.
A way of seeing.

Northbound is an invitation to experience Gilgit-Baltistan with more than a camera. To take the slower road, share a cup of chai, and see the land through the eyes of the people who call it home.

From the old forts of Hunza to the high pastures above Skardu, this is a place shaped by its landscape and the kindness of its communities. We want to help you discover both.

01

Travel with care for the mountains, wildlife, and everyday life of the valleys.

Leave space in the itinerary for a conversation, a detour, or another cup of chai.
